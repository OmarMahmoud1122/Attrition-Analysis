# Employee Attrition Analysis | Power BI

A 4-page interactive Power BI report that analyzes employee attrition to find who leaves, where, and why.

**What it covers**
- KPIs: total employees, number of leavers, attrition rate
- Breakdowns by department, job role, age group, income, overtime, and tenure
- AI visuals (Decomposition Tree and Key Influencers) to identify the main drivers

**Key findings**
- Most leavers earned under 10K a month
- Sales, and Sales Representatives in particular, have the highest attrition
- The 25–34 age group has the highest attrition rate
- Attrition drops as total working years increase

**Recommendations:** review pay for low-income roles, focus retention on Sales, reduce overtime, and support younger employees with mentoring and development.

**Tools:** Power BI · Power Query · DAX
